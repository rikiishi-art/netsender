NetSender Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

NetSender ("the app") is a tool for sending OSC, UDP and DMX (Art-Net / sACN) messages over your local Wi-Fi network. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Settings you enter (destination IP address, ports, DMX channels, send history, language) are saved only on your device. They are never transmitted to us. Deleting the app removes this data.

Local network

The app uses your local network only to send the messages you choose, to the IP address you enter. No data is sent to us or to any third party.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment information. Please see Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchase data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

For questions, please leave a review on the App Store page of NetSender.
